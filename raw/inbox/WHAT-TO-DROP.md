# What to drop here

A prioritised list of documents to gather, by lifecycle stage. You do not need all of
them — ingest what exists, and the gaps become open questions the wiki tracks for you.

**Drop one source at a time and say `ingest raw/inbox/<file>`.** Batch-dropping is
supported (`batch ingest`), but one at a time keeps you in the loop while the
conventions are still settling.

## Sales
- Qualification criteria / discovery call script or agenda
- Proposal or SOW template
- Pricing sheet, rate card, or discount rules
- The contract template, or a representative signed contract
- Anything describing what makes a lead a bad fit

## Onboarding
- Welcome email, intake form, or kickoff deck
- Kickoff call agenda
- Access / credential checklist
- Whatever defines when a client is considered "onboarded"

## Delivery
- Per-service-line process docs — one per offer
- Quality review or approval checklists
- Revision and feedback handling rules
- Reporting cadence and report templates
- Escalation paths when delivery slips

## Internal
- Invoicing and collections process; payment terms
- Hiring, contractor onboarding, and offboarding
- Tool stack list, with which system owns which source of truth
- Recurring internal meeting cadence and their agendas

## Also worth capturing
- Retro notes and post-mortems — these carry the *why*, and become `wiki/decisions/`
- Slack threads where a process decision was actually made
- Anything currently out of date. Say so on ingest and I will record what changed
  rather than silently overwriting.

## Highest-value first
If you want the fastest useful wiki: start with the **service-line delivery doc for
your largest offer**, then its **quality checklist**, then **onboarding**. Delivery is
where inconsistency costs the most, and the handoff seams surface immediately.
