Internal notes — onboarding handoff discussion
Date: 2026-09-10
Present: ops lead, account manager

Notes:

- When a contract is signed, sales drops the deal in the shared channel and the
  account manager picks it up. Sometimes this gets missed if it lands on a Friday.
- AM sends the welcome email same day if possible. Contains: intro, what happens next,
  and the intake form link.
- Intake form collects brand assets, access credentials, and who the day-to-day contact
  is on the client side.
- Kickoff call gets booked within the first week. Runs about 45 minutes. We used to do
  30 but it was too tight.
- Before kickoff, someone needs to set up the client workspace and invite the client
  contacts. Not always clear who does this — sometimes AM, sometimes ops.
- After kickoff, AM writes up a recap and sends it to the client. Should go out within
  24 hours but often slips.
- First invoice goes out after kickoff, not before. Terms are on the contract.
- Delivery team takes over after the recap is sent. AM stays on as the relationship
  owner.

Problems raised:
- No consistent definition of "onboarded". People disagree about whether it ends at
  kickoff or at first delivery.
- Access credentials sometimes arrive incomplete and nobody chases them.
